             GUILD DISTRIBUTION CODE

Distributions take place during the Guild Distribution Night, after the Thursday Guild Bosses, unless exceptional circumstances require the Staff to handle them differently.

I. GUILD COMMITMENT & PRIORITY

When certain weapons or equipment are considered strategically important for the Guild's PvP activities, the Staff may give priority to members who consistently participate in PvP events and actively contribute to the Guild's objectives.

A strategic item is any equipment that significantly improves the performance of members who regularly attend PvP content, since the benefits obtained from it ultimately strengthen the entire Guild. Examples include the buffs earned from Boonstones and Riftstones, as well as improved access to drops that benefit everyone.

This is not intended as a punishment or an obligation for players who prefer a more relaxed playstyle. Instead, it is a way to invest the Guild's resources where they provide the greatest benefit for the Guild as a whole.

We are not a hardcore guild, but we are also more than just a place to collect rewards. Everyone is free to play at their own pace, but those who consistently represent the Guild on the battlefield directly contribute to its growth and may therefore receive priority for strategic distributions.

Our goal has always been the same:
grow together, have fun together, and gradually become a Guild that enjoys challenging itself.
II. BOSS DROPS (PEACE & WAR)

Items dropped from Guild Bosses belong to the player who obtained them.

The player is free to:

Keep the item for personal use.
Donate it to the Guild.
Give it to another Guild member.

III. ARCHBOSS DROPS (PEACE & WAR)

Archboss equipment is considered rare and highly valuable.

Archboss items are distributed through a Roll among eligible candidates on a day separate from Distribution Night, within 5 days of the drop.

Participation Requirements

Only members who:

use the weapon in their Main Build;
have posted their build in the designated build channel;
have not changed their build solely to participate in the Roll.
Usage Confirmation

Players who receive an Archboss weapon must demonstrate that they are using it by providing proof showing the weapon with at least one Trait applied.

Archboss Accessories

If no eligible member requests an Archboss accessory, the player who obtained it may:

keep it;
donate it to the Guild.
No Eligible Candidates

If there are no eligible candidates, the item may be sold and the proceeds will be added to the Guild Fund.
IV. MAIN & SECONDARY BUILDS

Every member must publish and keep their Main Build updated.

The declared build represents the player's chosen progression path (PvE or PvP) and determines which items they are eligible to request.

Completed Build

A Main Build is considered complete once all equipment listed in the declared build has been obtained, regardless of enhancement level.

Secondary Build

After completing the Main Build, a member may start working on a Secondary Build.

The Secondary Build becomes eligible for Guild distributions only after the Main Build has been completed.

Choose carefully which build you want to prioritize.

Any build changes must be communicated to the Staff.

V. CANDIDATE EVALUATION

The Staff maintains an internal record containing:

Declared Builds
Wishlists
Distributed equipment
Build changes
Archboss drops
Overall character progression

This information is used only to verify:

Roll eligibility;
consistency between declared builds and requested items;
compliance with this Distribution Code.

It is never used to determine the winner of a Roll.
VI. ROTATION & DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM

During Distribution Night, items are assigned individually through a Roll.

Each item has its own independent Roll.

The Rotation System exists to distribute upgrades fairly during the same distribution session, allowing as many members as possible to receive at least one upgrade.

Categories

Items are divided into:

Weapons
Armor
Accessories
Skill Cores
Lithographs

Rotation applies separately to each category.

Rotation Rule

After receiving an item from a specific category, a member enters the rotation for that category and cannot participate in additional Rolls within that same category until the first distribution round has been completed.

They may still participate normally in Rolls for all other categories.

Exceptions

If:

there are no other eligible candidates, members already in rotation may participate;
there is only one eligible candidate, the item is awarded directly without a Roll.
VII. NEW MEMBERS

New members have a 14-day integration period starting from the day they join the Guild.

During this period they may:

participate in Guild activities;
join Guild Boss events;
complete and publish their Build and Wishlist.

After 14 days, they gain full access to the Guild distribution system.
VIII. GUILD FUND

Items that are not requested by any eligible member will be sold.

The proceeds are added to the Guild Fund, which exists to support the Guild's overall progression.

The Guild Fund may be used to:

help members who need Lucent for specific purchases;
support Guild progression and collective growth.

The available balance will be announced in the dedicated channel.

Requests will be evaluated during Distribution Night based on:

necessity;
available Guild Funds;
fairness among members.

The Guild Fund is not a reservation system.

Whenever an item is successfully sold, it will be announced in the ⁠help-me-lucent channel. Requests can only be considered after the available Guild Fund has been officially announced.

IX.GENERAL PROVISIONS**
The Staff may intervene only in exceptional situations not covered by this Distribution Code.

Any decision must be justified and based on the principles of:

transparency;
fairness;
the best interest of the Guild.

Whenever multiple eligible candidates exist, the Roll remains the only objective method of determining the recipient.

X. FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE

The strength of Nyxミ does not come from one player's luck, but from the collective growth of the Guild.

Every decision is made with the Guild's success placed above individual interests.

Nyxミ grows together.
